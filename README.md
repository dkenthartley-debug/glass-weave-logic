# HLG Glass Innovations

Build a professional website concept for Hotlineglass USA / HLG, a specialty glass technology company focused on conductive interlayers, embedded wire products, electrically heated glass systems, aircraft windshield sensors, EMI/RFI/SCIF shielding glass inserts, transparent heating, defrosting, de-icing, variable tint, ADAS glazing support, transparent antenna concepts, and specialty laminated glass solutions.

Company positioning:
HLG is not a commodity glass fabricator. It develops and supplies specialty interlayers, conductive films, embedded wire products, sensor mats, heater mats, bus bar systems, and production-ready laminated glass subassemblies for advanced glazing manufacturers, aerospace suppliers, defense programs, SCIF/security contractors, transportation glazing companies, and industrial OEMs.

Preferred language and positioning:
Use “Conductive Interlayers & Specialty Films” as the core positioning. Avoid the word “functional” as the primary descriptor. Emphasize conductive, embedded, engineered, production-ready, repeatable, and integration-focused solutions. The company helps customers integrate electrical capability into laminated glass systems.

Suggested tagline options to incorporate:
1. Conductive Interlayers for Advanced Glass Systems
2. Embedded Conductive Solutions for Laminated Glass
3. Specialty Interlayers, Films & Sensors for Electrified Glass
4. Practical Conductive Glass Solutions for Aerospace, Defense & Specialty Glazing

Primary homepage headline:
Conductive Interlayers & Specialty Films for Advanced Laminated Glass Systems

Homepage subhead:
HLG develops and supplies specialty interlayers, embedded wire products, conductive films, sensor mats, and electrically active glass components that help customers build heated, shielded, sensing, and specialty laminated glass systems in a practical, repeatable, production-ready format.

Design preferences:
Clean industrial aerospace/defense style. Dark navy, charcoal, silver, white, and subtle electric blue accents. High-trust B2B look. Avoid consumer-style stock photography. Use technical diagrams, abstract glass layers, conductive grid patterns, aircraft/defense/industrial themes, and modular sections. The tone should be concise, technical, credible, and operator-driven.

Homepage structure:
1. Hero section with headline, subhead, and CTA buttons: “Discuss a Project” and “View Capabilities”
2. Brief explanation of what HLG does
3. Three main solution cards: Conductive Interlayers, Aircraft Sensors & Heated Glass, EMI/RFI/SCIF Shielding
4. Applications grid
5. Why HLG: practical process knowledge, laminated glass experience, conductive integration, prototype-to-production support
6. Technical capability highlights
7. Contact section for engineering, supplier development, and project inquiries

Contact CTA:
Have a conductive glass, heated glass, sensor, or shielding requirement? Contact HLG to discuss feasibility, materials, samples, and production path.

Do not make unsupported claims such as certified supplier, FAA approved, sole-source, patented, or guaranteed performance unless phrased as target applications or development capabilities. Keep claims credible and careful.
